Conventional cotton uses roughly 16% of the world's insecticides and 7% of its pesticides — despite being grown on just 2.4% of the world's farmland. Those numbers are hard to ignore when you're making clothes.

## Why we switched

When we started The Break Surf, we knew we wanted to make things properly. That meant thinking about the full picture — not just how a garment performs on the trail, but what went into making it. Organic cotton was the obvious starting point.

GOTS-certified organic cotton is grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilisers, using methods that support soil health and reduce water consumption. The certification also covers the entire supply chain — so we know the mills processing our fabric meet the same standards.

## What it costs

The honest truth is that organic cotton costs more. Our Boulder Tee takes a hit on margin compared to a conventional equivalent. But we'd rather make fewer things at a higher standard than flood the market with cheap kit that ends up in landfill after a season.

## How it feels

The fabric itself is noticeably different — it has a weight and softness that comes from not being stripped by chemicals during processing. Once you've worn a properly made organic cotton tee, it's hard to go back.

We're not claiming to be perfect. There's still a long way to go in understanding the full impact of everything we make. But using organic cotton is one concrete step we can point to and say: **this is better, and we can prove it.**
