When I started The Break, I knew that the pillars of the company ethos would be community, environment, and creativity. I wanted an outlet to express my own creativity and to champion others; at the same time, I wanted to highlight the importance of nature and outdoors exposure in an ever-growing digital world. Recent years have seen a massive rise in smartphone use, from preteens all the way up to OAPs, and at The Break, we wanted to push people towards living more natural lives.

The final pillar was perhaps the most important one. **The environment**.

Of course, the materials we use are all organic and recycled, the shipping bags are compostable, the promotional stickers are vegan, and any card we use is biodegradable. But we didn't want to stop there — we wanted to go even further. Eventually, we moved our website servers away from large data centres and onto small IoT devices sitting in a cupboard in the flat, reusing old technology and cheap hardware to run power-efficient micro servers. (Eventually, we plan to hook up solar panels and run the entire thing using renewables.)

Ultimately, we decided that being net-zero was not enough; we wanted to be proactive. Starting on the 1st of January 2026, we announced that we'd be undertaking monthly litter picks. **Since then, we have achieved seven months' worth of picks and are excited to grow our group even more**.

Some days, we have plenty of people join us; other days, it's just myself and Archie, my co-founder. Either way, they are always an incredible way to start the day and, above all else, a good excuse to chat and catch up whilst doing something for the planet. If we're on the beach, we tend to end with a dip; if not, we often try to find a coffee place for afterwards.

If you want to get involved, or know a spot that could do with some attention, drop us a message. The more people, the more ground we can cover.

**The latest information will always be posted on the Events page** and across our social channels.
